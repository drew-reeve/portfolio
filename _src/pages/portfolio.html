September 2026 · Lewis & Clark Trail Adventures for the Lewis & Clark Trail Alliance

2026 Lolo Trail Condition Assessment Report Case study

A five-person team walked and scouted Lewis and Clark Trail #25 from Glade Creek, near Lolo Pass, west toward the Weippe Prairie. The report rates every segment on a five-point condition scale, maps GPS waypoints, compares results with a parallel 2026 survey, and recommends markers, volunteer projects and professional crew work for 2027.

My part: field assessment and GPS data, the segment inventory worksheet, and writing and compiling the final report.

  • GPS waypoints
