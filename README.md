# drew-reeve.github.io/portfolio

Personal site of Andrew "Drew" Reeve: economist, data scientist and river & trail guide in Lolo, Montana.

- `index.html` home · `portfolio.html` all work · `work/` case studies · `cv.html` CV (PDF in `docs/`)
- Pages are generated from `_src/pages/` by `python3 _src/build.py` (shared header, footer, photo markup).
- Photos: `_src/photos.py` lists every photo with its caption; `_src/process_photos.py` resizes and grades originals into `img/p/`.
- Contact form posts to FormSubmit (no backend).
- Fonts: Zilla Slab, Public Sans, IBM Plex Mono, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License (`fonts/`).
